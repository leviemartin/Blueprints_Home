## Goal
bedroom_precool.yaml knows, on every tick, whether today is a cool, heat or neutral day (season_mode auto/cool/heat, October-April window keyed to the night's start date, cooling checked first, a running cool/dry unit latches cool and a running heat unit latches heat), the heating phase targets and the capped heating setpoints. Before any forecast I/O it shuts down on vacation and enforces the heat guards: inside the heat season, a unit running heat is switched off when the warmest room is above phase target + tolerance or all bedroom sensors fail; outside the heat season, a unit observed running heat above that threshold (or with all sensors failed) is corrected to cool at the cooling maintaining setpoint in one command (Martin 2026-11-03). A guard tick still runs the STEP 5c fan actions (fan safety first, Martin 2026-11-03) and then stops before the AC dispatch. The cool-forcing heat backstop is off on heat days and on guard ticks, and heating setpoints count as known setpoints whenever heating is possible. Known limitation (accepted, Decided by planner (a)): a heat-season guard stop inside the fan_only lock's 180 s exemption is not a manual_off; cards 2 and 3 carry a test for it. Cooling-day behaviour is otherwise unchanged; no evening, night or morning heating command and no heat-mode command is added (cards 2-4).

## Bad inputs to handle
- season_mode outside auto, cool, heat: rejected by the selector, and season_mode_safe falls back to auto.
- heat_hall_offset above 4 or below 0 or non-numeric: clamped to 0-4, non-numeric gives 2.
- heat_threshold, evening_target, morning_target or night_floor non-numeric or empty: the defaults 19, 20, 20, 17 apply.
- Target + heat_hall_offset outside 16-30 C or off the 0.5 grid: the setpoint is clamped to 16-30 C and the unit's own min/max, floored to the 0.5 step, int() on a whole-degree unit.
- Bedroom sensors all unavailable, unknown or missing while a unit runs heat: in the heat season it is switched off; outside it, it is corrected to cool at the maintaining setpoint; either way the fan step still runs and the run then stops before the AC dispatch; heating_allowed is false.
- A unit running heat above phase target + tolerance outside the heat season (summer auto, forced cool, ac_hold): one set_temperature with hvac_mode cool at the maintaining setpoint, no turn_off, so no manual_off.
- That correction lost or only half applied: only a reported exit from heat ends it. While the device reports heat at any other setpoint it is re-sent on every tick; while it reports heat at the safe setpoint it is retried only on 5-minute ticks (now().minute % 5 == 0, as guard_due at line 1288), at most 12 attempts/h; once it reports cool (or any non-heat mode), no further command. The 5-minute/12-per-hour limit covers only this correction branch: on the other ticks the v1.2.1 fall-through stays as is (heat_backstop_due on fan_only nights, PRECOOL's mode guard), each re-sending its own cool/dry mode command.
- Forecast response null, scalar, string, mapping, empty mapping, `forecast: []`, or entries without a datetime or numeric temperature: forecast_low falls back to the outdoor reading; with the outdoor reading also unavailable it is none and the forecast clause never fires.
- Night start date on 30 April or 1 May, and 30 September or 1 October: the October-April window is keyed to the night's start date (pivot at wake_time).
- Vacation turned on, or manual override (setpoint or off) detected, while heating runs: heating_allowed is false; vacation switches the unit off before any forecast I/O.
- cooling_needed flipping between the 15-minute hourly window and the daily high, or an unavailable->cool cloud flap, on a cooling unit: season stays cool (cool latch).
- A stalled or failing forecast fetch: vacation, the heat guard and the off-season correction all run before every weather.get_forecasts call (guard calls carry continue_on_error so a failed call does not abort the run).
- A guard tick (heat stop or off-season correction, including every-tick retries of a lost correction at an unsafe setpoint): the fan step still runs, so fans_unsafe_on cuts an interlocked fan within its window and fans_at_wake off still fires at wake.
