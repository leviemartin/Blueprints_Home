### The 0.30 rule (the only path above 0.25)
Only `script.livingroom_music_volume` can send more than 0.25. It sends 0.30 only when the index is 5 and, checked inside the script at the call, kids on, kids timer active, approved on and approval session = kids session. The index reaches 5 only through the volume script itself, on a remote up at index 4 or an accepted Approve, each passing the kids session it checked; the script drops the run when that session is no longer current. approved turns on only in the answer automation; kids start and kids end turn it off and bump the session, so a late grant or press can never count. The reset's ladder stops at 0.25, the one queue orders every ladder write (the fresh start's 0.15 included) ahead of kids end's reset, and a reset sent past the queue never clears a debt while a ladder write is in flight. The volume script checks the session and the live approval again directly before the volume_set. Card remote-volume's test 2 (as edited in step 7) and tests 21 to 31, 36, 37, 42, 43 and 44 prove it.

### Rare corners (accepted, no extra rule)
- An Approve tapped while the pushes are still being sent is ignored; the request stays open for a second tap.
- A Sonos-app pause during kids time, then a stop rule: the pause automation does not run (Sonos not playing), so kids time ends at the 30 min timer with only the volume reset; the paused Pairilijst is replaced by a fresh Lazy Sunday on the next start edge.
- With Hands-off on at kids end, Pairilijst keeps playing at the pre-kids level (only the volume reset is sent).
- A restart with a speaker absent ends kids time without a fresh start; Pairilijst may keep playing at the pre-kids level until the next start edge after it stops.
- A restart with a debt and kids off restores the pre-kids level while the step comes back at 2; the next press resyncs both speakers.
- A debt carried into a later kids time can lower the owing speaker to the pre-kids level while the other plays louder; the next press resyncs both, and that kids end sets both debts again.
- A core fresh start right after a kids end sends its 0.15 through the same queue; if it lands after the reset, music starts at the normal 0.15 start level.
- A dim or brighten pressed in the instant kids time starts or ends (triggered before the session bump, run after it) is dropped; the next press works.
- A stopped reset attempt's call may still land after a later dim (executor thread); it sends only the pre-kids level, and the next press resyncs both speakers.
- A press whose ladder run starts after kids end's reset was queued but while kids end is still in its playback steps sends its level (0.25 or less), writes no step, clears no debt, and then re-sends the pre-kids reset.
- Kids end never stops an in-flight kids start. They overlap only in the roughly one second after an On press, when an early stop rule or Hands-off ends kids time; a stalled Pairilijst play_media may then land after kids end's fresh Lazy Sunday reload. The next stop or start edge corrects it; the volume reset is unaffected.
- Interleavings of one press with kids end (each ends with step = pre-kids step and the pre-kids reset after any level the press sent): (1) press before the session bump: dropped by the up-front session check, or it started before kids end, so kids end's reset queues behind it, and its step write is overwritten or reconciled; (2) press between kids off and the session bump, or (3) after the bump while kids end runs: no step write, its volume_set (≤ 0.25) is followed by its own re-sent reset and kids end's queued reset; (4) press after kids end finished: an ordinary press from the pre-kids step.

### Accepted P3 (not fixed)
- Security r8 P2 #2, accepted as P3 by Martin's final-round decision: restarting a stalled reset attempt cancels its await but not the Sonos executor thread, so repeated stalls can leave threads running. soco's own request timeout could not be verified in the venv. The coder adds nothing for it.

### Risks
- `context.id` must differ per request run; test 23 checks it. If not, the coder uses `now().timestamp()` plus the kids session in the id and says so.
- The `failed` trigger depends on the request script's state turning off after an error; test 29 proves it. A kids-end turn_off also fires it, which is harmless.
- The core and remote-volume tests must stay green after steps 2, 3 and 5; a failing one means an edit went beyond the listed changes.
